# Aside v1.22 — eight stories, and a choice that is remembered

**Download `Aside-linux-x64.tar.gz` (Linux, one file, no Java needed) or `aside.html` (every story that has a web
build, in one page).**

## Something to notice that is not a story

Three of these eight are built differently from the thirty-five before them, and you will feel it rather than see it.

**A choice is now remembered.** Until this week every story here was a tree: you decided at one crux and the ending
followed. In **Half Term**, whether you write the boiler pump up and put it in front of the head changes *which endings
you can reach* at all — the ending where the head knows something is **unreachable unless you said something**. In
**Called Home**, saying yes to calling the funeral touch splits the last practice into **two different evenings**, so
the middle of the story is a different scene depending on an earlier answer. And in **Fish on a Friday**, the option to
shut at nine and change the oil before going home — *the option to put it right* — **appears only if you did not put it
right this morning.** In **Cone Ten** the ramp you chose at midnight is still deciding what the glaze looks like a week
later.

**One of these stories is not told to you.** Forty-two of them put you inside the trade — *you* have been frying here
for twenty-two years. **Out of Season** is told in the third person: you watch Marion run a guest house through an
October, and the choices are hers. It is the first story here since **Two of Everything** to do that, and it reads
differently on purpose.

**And one is denser.** **Cone Ten** carries 122 words a scene against the library's usual 82 — half again as much on
each screen — which makes it a quicker, tighter read than anything else here.

## The eight

- **Fourteen Inches** — a thatcher, a ridge nine years early, and *the last man's work* found under it: **every spar
  notched a quarter of an inch too deep, which is not a mistake you make with a chisel by accident, it is a mistake
  you make by doing it faster.** Four endings, and ringing the man who did it is one of them.
- **One Dog Short** — nine hundred ewes on two hills and one dog left. Moss is nine and a half and going blind; his
  nose is perfect and his ears are perfect and he works the near hill exactly as he always has, **because he knows it,
  and knowing it is not the same as seeing it and it is doing the work.**
- **Up the Entry** — a coal round in the last week before Christmas, a nineteen-year-old on his second winter, and three
  small decisions that accumulate. *"It's not for the ones that pay. It's for the ones that aren't in and have paid
  anyway."*
- **Half Term** — a school caretaker of nineteen years, a boiler pump with the word **DEFER** against it twice, and the
  question you have been dreading for eleven years: *"How long have you known about this one."*
- **Called Home** — a bell tower, a ringer of fifty-four years who has died, and whether anybody in the band can call a
  course of Plain Bob Major, half-muffled, at a funeral, **without it coming apart.**
- **Fish on a Friday** — a chip shop, twenty-two years of frying, and oil that is due out today. Four hundred pounds
  now, or a Friday and a Saturday on oil that has gone.
- **Out of Season** — a guest house holding eleven, down to three by the middle of October, and a man in room four who
  pays a week at a time in cash and does not leave. **Three weeks of one guest is three hundred and fifteen pounds.**
- **Cone Ten** — an updraught kiln older than the potter, fourteen hours of wood, four big jars, and a wind that
  arrives at four exactly as forecast.

All eight are audited rather than asserted: every scene reachable, every ending reachable, no choice leading nowhere,
no conditional option that no path can ever offer. That is **forty-three stories**.

## Eight stories cost 609 KB

`aside.html` is **10,194,326 bytes** against **9,570,154** for v1.21 — **609 kilobytes for eight more stories**, about
76 KB each, and still a page a phone can be asked to hold. **The phone builds carry no sound at all, deliberately**: no
inlined audio and no audio playback anywhere, because the shelf is ten megabytes of artwork already and sound for
forty-three stories is the size that was not worth paying. It said here because a silent build and a broken one look
identical.

## What is verified, and what is not

Every story is walked by the auditor above, including its art and its sound cues. The game builds are checked against
the generators that write them, the frames against the code that draws them, the audio against the scripts that
synthesise it, and the README's counts against the library itself — 44 suites, about 11,000 checks, and 39 self-tests
of the tools themselves.

**Nothing here can tell you whether a story lands.** Structure and reachability are checkable; whether a story is worth
reading is not, and no test in this repository claims otherwise.
