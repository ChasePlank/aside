Port Residue in as the fourth game -- the first one with no script

aside.games.residue -- one room, five things, one visit at a time. You
leave a single thing and go; when you come back the room has aged without
you. The mechanic is the difference between evidence and messages: a cup
you forgot outlasts a note you wrote on purpose, because a message is
about the writer and wanting is fragile.

This is the first game in the library that has no .aside file, and that
is the point. There is nothing to author and nothing to read ahead of --
the content is generated from the room's state, and the only thing that
differs between playthroughs is what the player did last time. It is also
the first game that owns its persistence rather than borrowing the
engine's: it keeps its own plain-text room in saves/residue/room.state,
because the thing being saved is not a position in a story.

The Game interface held. It has now survived a visual novel, a real-time
survival game, a platformer, and a thing with no script at all, without
gaining a method.

THE BUG THE PORT EXPOSED, in the new module rather than the engine:

ResidueGame.create() loaded the room and handed it to the screen without
aging it. The room ages on arrival, so the first frame showed "visit 0"
and traces at the ages they were saved at -- the room was displayed
before it had happened to you. Caught by rendering the screen rather than
by reading the code. Fixed to advance on create. It is in-memory only:
the room is written back when the player actually leaves something, so
walking in and out again costs nothing and reopening the screen twice
gives the same room both times.

Design note, since it is not obvious from the code: the room holds THREE
things, not five. Five things and five places would mean the choice of
what to leave is free. At three, leaving something new costs the oldest
thing in the room, and the player has to decide what the next person is
allowed to find.

Verified: the 46 room assertions run against the ported package, and all
four beats (arrival, pick, gesture, departure) were rendered headlessly
through the engine's own snapshot mode.
