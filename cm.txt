Let the shelf say what is not on it

The shelf was derived and honest about what it carried, and silent about what
it did not. Six cards read as the whole library, and the games absent from
them were exactly the ones a player could not tell were absent -- the four of
mine that are still desktop-only, plus the two that are Roxanne's.

So the same walk over Games.all() that fills the shelf now also produces the
games with no phone build, and they are listed underneath under their own
heading. Derived, not written down: a game that gets a build leaves that list
and joins the shelf in the same edit, and nobody has to remember to move it.

They are drawn as text and not as cards, because there is nothing on the other
end of them on a phone, and a card that looks tappable and is not is worse
than a list.

Checked, because the failure mode is the one the orphan check was written for:
the invariant is stated once -- every game in the library is exactly one of
on-the-shelf or named-as-missing -- and it is asserted for all eleven. Engine
suite 92 -> 116 checks.

Also: Testimony gets the note the other three persistent games have, since it
does keep its account in the browser now.
